# Analysis Summary — Identifying Shopping Trends

## Key Statistics

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Total Customers | 3,900 |
| Total Columns | 19 |
| Product Categories | 4 (Clothing, Footwear, Accessories, Outerwear) |
| Seasons Covered | 4 (Spring, Summer, Fall, Winter) |
| Average Review Rating | ~3.75 / 5.0 |

## Main Findings

### Customer Demographics
- The dataset covers customers across a wide age range, with the **25–45 age group** being the most active segment.
- **Male customers** represent the majority of transactions, but **female customers** tend to have a slightly higher average purchase amount.

### Category & Product Trends
- **Clothing** is the most purchased category, accounting for the largest share of transactions.
- **Accessories** and **Footwear** follow as secondary categories.
- Popular individual items include blouses, jewelry, and pants.

### Seasonal Patterns
- **Fall and Winter** drive the highest purchase volumes.
- **Spring** records the lowest transaction counts, indicating an opportunity for targeted promotions.

### Payment Behavior
- **Credit Card** is the most used payment method, followed by **PayPal** and **Cash**.
- Subscribed customers are more likely to use promo codes and receive discounts.

### Subscription Insights
- Subscribers demonstrate a **higher frequency of purchases** compared to non-subscribers.
- Promo code usage is nearly exclusive to subscribed customers.

## Business Insights

1. **High-Value Segment**: The 25–45 age group with subscription status drives the majority of revenue.
2. **Seasonal Opportunity**: Targeted Fall/Winter campaigns can capitalize on natural demand spikes.
3. **Loyalty Programs Work**: Subscription customers show measurably better engagement and spend.
4. **Regional Concentrations**: Certain US states outperform others — these merit deeper investigation and increased marketing investment.
5. **Category Depth**: Clothing's dominance suggests cross-selling opportunities into Accessories and Outerwear.

## Recommendations

| Priority | Action | Expected Impact |
|---|---|---|

| High | Expand and incentivize subscription/loyalty programs | Improve retention and purchase frequency |
